Why QuizlyNotes works well for exam revision

Re-reading notes feels productive but mostly builds familiarity. Answering questions forces you to recall information, and that retrieval is what makes it stick (the testing effect). Spacing reviews out over days, rather than cramming, strengthens it further.

QuizlyNotes is built around both ideas. Your material becomes topic-by-topic revision notes, then a quiz that mixes ten question types with an explanation for every answer, then flashcards scheduled with spaced repetition. You can try it on your own notes without an account.
