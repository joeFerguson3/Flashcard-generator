Question types

Quiz notes that test what you know

Every question is written from your material, never from the internet, and covers each topic in proportion to how much there is to learn. Pick the types you want, and the difficulty.

Made for lecture slides

Your whole slide deck is read at once, so labelled diagrams and tables become questions too. Module codes, image credits and repeated title slides are skipped.

Spaced repetition

Organised by subject

Every set is saved to your library under its subject, with your best and latest scores, so you can see what needs another go before the exam.
