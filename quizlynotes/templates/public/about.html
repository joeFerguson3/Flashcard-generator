How it works

  1. Upload a PDF, lecture slides, a Word or PowerPoint file, or paste your notes.
  2. QuizlyNotes writes revision notes grouped by topic. You can edit them, or use Simplify to keep only the key points.
  3. It writes a quiz from those notes, with the question types and difficulty you choose, and a deck of flashcards.
  4. You study topic by topic, see an explanation for every answer, and review flashcards when they're due.
